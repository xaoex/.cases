# Legal Filings

## Purpose

This directory contains all legal documents filed with courts or administrative bodies, as well as documents received from opposing parties.

## Organization

```
legal-filings/
├── complaints/           # Initial complaints and petitions
├── answers/             # Defendant responses
├── motions/             # All motions filed or received
├── discovery/           # Discovery requests and responses
├── briefs/              # Legal briefs and memoranda
├── orders/              # Court orders and decisions
├── transcripts/         # Hearing and deposition transcripts
└── appeals/             # Appellate documents
```

## Document Standards

### File Naming Convention

```
[YYYY-MM-DD]_[CASE-ID]_[DOCTYPE]_[DESCRIPTION].[ext]

Examples:
2024-01-15_CASE001_COMPLAINT_Initial-Filing.pdf
2024-02-20_CASE001_MOTION_Summary-Judgment.pdf
2024-03-10_CASE001_ORDER_Granting-Discovery.pdf
```

### Version Control

For documents going through drafts:
```
[YYYY-MM-DD]_[CASE-ID]_[DOCTYPE]_[DESCRIPTION]_v[N].[ext]

Examples:
2024-01-10_CASE001_COMPLAINT_Initial-Filing_v1.docx
2024-01-12_CASE001_COMPLAINT_Initial-Filing_v2.docx
2024-01-15_CASE001_COMPLAINT_Initial-Filing_FINAL.pdf
```

## Complaints and Petitions

### Contents

A complaint typically includes:

1. **Caption** - Court, parties, case number
2. **Jurisdiction and Venue** - Authority and location
3. **Parties** - Identification of all parties
4. **Factual Allegations** - Numbered paragraphs stating facts
5. **Causes of Action** - Legal claims (as separate counts)
6. **Prayer for Relief** - What plaintiff seeks
7. **Jury Demand** - If applicable
8. **Signature and Verification** - Attorney/party signature

### Filing Requirements

Before filing:
- [ ] Check court rules for formatting requirements
- [ ] Verify filing fees
- [ ] Prepare required number of copies
- [ ] Complete certificate of service
- [ ] Obtain any required certifications
- [ ] Review for confidential information
- [ ] Verify statute of limitations compliance

## Answers and Responsive Pleadings

### Contents

An answer typically includes:

1. **Admissions and Denials** - Response to each allegation
2. **Affirmative Defenses** - Defenses raised by defendant
3. **Counterclaims** - Claims against plaintiff (if any)
4. **Cross-claims** - Claims against co-defendants (if any)

### Deadlines

Track answer deadlines carefully:
- **Federal Court:** Generally 21 days after service
- **State Court:** Varies by jurisdiction
- **Extensions:** Must be requested before deadline

## Motions

### Types of Motions

#### Pre-Trial Motions
- Motion to Dismiss
- Motion for Summary Judgment
- Motion for More Definite Statement
- Motion to Strike
- Motion to Compel Discovery
- Motion for Protective Order
- Motion in Limine

#### Trial Motions
- Motion for Directed Verdict
- Motion for Judgment as a Matter of Law

#### Post-Trial Motions
- Motion for New Trial
- Motion for Judgment Notwithstanding Verdict
- Motion to Alter or Amend Judgment

### Motion Components

A motion typically includes:

1. **Notice of Motion** - States what is being requested
2. **Memorandum of Law** - Legal argument supporting motion
3. **Supporting Evidence** - Declarations, affidavits, exhibits
4. **Proposed Order** - Draft order for judge to sign

### Motion Practice

- **File and serve** motion on all parties
- **Opposing party response** deadline (varies by court)
- **Reply** deadline (if applicable)
- **Hearing** date (if required)
- **Order** issued by court

## Discovery

### Types of Discovery

#### Interrogatories
Written questions requiring written answers under oath

#### Requests for Production
Demands for documents and tangible things

#### Requests for Admission
Requests to admit or deny specific facts

#### Depositions
Oral testimony under oath, recorded

#### Subpoenas
Compulsory process for documents or testimony

### Discovery Index

Maintain an index of:

### Discovery Deadlines

Track deadlines rigorously:
- **Response time** - Typically 30 days
- **Discovery cutoff** - End of discovery period
- **Extension requests** - File before deadline
- **Supplementation** - Duty to update responses

## Briefs and Memoranda

### Types

- **Motion Briefs** - Support or oppose motions
- **Trial Briefs** - Outline trial position
- **Appellate Briefs** - Appeal or respond to appeal

### Brief Structure

1. **Table of Contents** - For longer briefs
2. **Table of Authorities** - Cases, statutes, rules cited
3. **Statement of the Case** - Procedural history
4. **Statement of Facts** - Relevant facts
5. **Argument** - Legal analysis with headings
6. **Conclusion** - Summary of requested relief

### Citation Format

Follow court rules on citation format:
- **Federal Courts** - Usually Bluebook or ALWD
- **State Courts** - Check local rules
- **Consistency** - Use same format throughout

- **Dispositive Orders** - Summary judgment, dismissal

## Appeals

### Appellate Documents

- **Notice of Appeal** - Initiates appeal
- **Appellant's Brief** - Arguing for reversal
- **Appellee's Brief** - Defending judgment
- **Reply Brief** - Appellant's response
- **Appendix** - Record excerpts

### Appeal Deadlines

**CRITICAL:** Appeal deadlines are jurisdictional

- **Notice of Appeal** - Often 30 days from judgment
- **Record Assembly** - Varies by court
- **Brief Filing** - Per appellate court schedule
- **Oral Argument** - If granted

### Appeal Standards

- **Issues Preserved** - Must have objected at trial
- **Standard of Review** - Varies by issue type
  - De novo (legal issues)
  - Clear error (factual findings)
  - Abuse of discretion (discretionary decisions)

## Document Management

### Filing System

- **Organize chronologically** and by document type
- **Index all documents** for quick reference
- **Cross-reference** related documents
- **Update regularly** as new filings occur

### Access Control

- **Public filings** - Available to anyone
- **Confidential filings** - Restricted access
- **Sealed documents** - Court order required
- **Privileged documents** - Attorney work product

### Retention

- **Active cases** - Retain all documents
- **Closed cases** - Follow retention schedule
- **Appeals period** - Retain beyond closure
- **Permanent records** - Key documents to preserve
