# Legal Reference Guide

This document provides quick reference to commonly cited laws and regulations for cases in EU, US, and Swedish jurisdictions.

---

## European Union Law

### General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
**Regulation (EU) 2016/679**

#### Key Articles

| Article | Title | Summary |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Art. 5 | Principles | Lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity and confidentiality |
| Art. 6 | Lawfulness of processing | Legal bases for processing personal data |
| Art. 7 | Conditions for consent | Requirements for valid consent |
| Art. 15 | Right of access | Right to access personal data |
| Art. 16 | Right to rectification | Right to correct inaccurate data |
| Art. 17 | Right to erasure | "Right to be forgotten" |
| Art. 20 | Right to data portability | Right to receive and transfer data |
| Art. 77 | Right to lodge complaint | Right to complain to supervisory authority |
| Art. 82 | Right to compensation | Right to compensation for damages |

**Penalties:** Up to €20 million or 4% of annual global turnover, whichever is higher

### Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union
- **Article 7:** Respect for private and family life
- **Article 8:** Protection of personal data
- **Article 47:** Right to an effective remedy and fair trial

### EU Consumer Rights Directive
**Directive 2011/83/EU**
- Right to information
- Right of withdrawal (14 days)
- Delivery obligations

### EU E-Commerce Directive
**Directive 2000/31/EC**
- Information requirements for service providers
- Liability of intermediary service providers

## United States Federal Law

### Constitutional Rights

#### First Amendment
- Freedom of speech
- Freedom of press
- Right to petition

#### Fourth Amendment
- Protection against unreasonable searches and seizures
- Privacy rights

#### Fifth Amendment
- Due process
- Protection against self-incrimination
- Double jeopardy protection

#### Fourteenth Amendment
- Equal protection under law
- Due process (state level)

### Privacy and Data Protection

#### Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)
**18 U.S.C. § 1030**
- Unauthorized access to computers
- Exceeding authorized access
- Fraud related to computers

#### Electronic Communications Privacy Act (ECPA)
**18 U.S.C. §§ 2510-2522**
- **Title I (Wiretap Act):** Interception of communications
- **Title II (Stored Communications Act):** Stored electronic communications
- **Title III:** Pen registers and trap and trace devices

#### Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
**15 U.S.C. §§ 6501-6506**
- Protection of children's online privacy (under 13)

#### California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
**Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.**
- Right to know about personal data collected
- Right to delete personal data
- Right to opt-out of sale of personal data
- Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

#### Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)
- Protection of health information

### Intellectual Property

#### Copyright Act
**17 U.S.C. § 101 et seq.**
- Copyright protection
- Fair use (17 U.S.C. § 107)
- DMCA (17 U.S.C. § 512)

#### Lanham Act (Trademark)
**15 U.S.C. § 1051 et seq.**
- Trademark registration and protection

### Consumer Protection

#### Federal Trade Commission Act
**15 U.S.C. § 45**
- Unfair or deceptive business practices

### Tort Law

#### Common Law Torts
- **Defamation:** Libel and slander
- **Invasion of Privacy:** 
  - Intrusion upon seclusion
  - Public disclosure of private facts
  - False light
  - Appropriation of name or likeness
- **Intentional Infliction of Emotional Distress**
- **Negligence**

## Swedish Law

### Constitutional Law

#### Regeringsformen (RF) - Instrument of Government
- **Kapitel 1:** Grundläggande bestämmelser (Basic provisions)
- **Kapitel 2:** Grundläggande fri- och rättigheter (Fundamental freedoms and rights)

#### Tryckfrihetsförordningen (TF) - Freedom of the Press Act
- Freedom of expression and information

#### Yttrandefrihetsgrundlagen (YGL) - Fundamental Law on Freedom of Expression
- Freedom of expression in media

### Criminal Law

#### Brottsbalken (BrB) - Swedish Penal Code

##### Key Chapters
| Chapter | Title (Swedish/English) | Key Sections |
|---------|------------------------|--------------|
| Kap. 3 | Brott mot liv och hälsa / Crimes against life and health | Assault, battery |
| Kap. 4 | Brott mot frihet och frid / Crimes against liberty and peace | Unlawful threat, unlawful coercion |
| Kap. 5 | Ärekränkningsbrott / Defamation | Libel, slander, insult |
| Kap. 6 | Sexualbrott / Sexual offenses | Sexual assault, harassment |
| Kap. 8 | Stöld, rån och andra tillgreppsbrott / Theft, robbery | Theft, robbery, embezzlement |
| Kap. 9 | Bedrägeri och annan oredlighet / Fraud | Fraud, misrepresentation |
| Kap. 16 | Brott mot allmän ordning / Crimes against public order | Data intrusion |
| Kap. 20 | Förfalskningsbrott / Forgery | Document forgery |

**4 kap. 5 § BrB - Olaga hot (Unlawful threat)**  
**4 kap. 4 § BrB - Olaga tvång (Unlawful coercion)**  
**5 kap. 1 § BrB - Förtal (Defamation)**

### Data Protection

#### Dataskyddslagen (2018:218)
- Implements GDPR in Swedish law
- Supplementary provisions to GDPR

#### Personuppgiftslagen (PuL) - Superseded by Dataskyddslagen
- Historical reference for cases before 2018

### Civil Law

#### Skadeståndslagen (1972:207) - Tort Liability Act
- **2 §:** Personal injury
- **3 §:** Damages for infringement of privacy
- **5 §:** Damages for financial loss

#### Marknadsföringslagen (2008:486) - Marketing Practices Act
- Unfair commercial practices
- Misleading marketing

### Consumer Protection

#### Konsumentköplagen (1990:932) - Consumer Sales Act
- Rights in consumer sales

#### Distans- och hemförsäljningslagen (2005:59) - Distance and Off-Premises Contracts Act
- Right of withdrawal

### Labor Law

#### Arbetsmiljölagen (1977:1160) - Work Environment Act
- Employer obligations for safe workplace

#### Diskrimineringslagen (2008:567) - Discrimination Act
- Protection against discrimination

### Procedural Law

#### Rättegångsbalken (RB) - Code of Judicial Procedure
- Civil procedure
- Criminal procedure
- Evidence rules

## Citation Formats

### EU Law
- Regulations: Regulation (EU) No XXX/YYYY
- Directives: Directive YYYY/XX/EU
- Articles: Article X, Article X(Y)

### US Law
- Federal Statutes: XX U.S.C. § YYYY
- Case Law: Case Name, XXX U.S. YYY (Year) or XXX F.Xd YYY (Circuit Year)
- Constitutional: U.S. Const. amend. X

### Swedish Law
- Statutes: [Short name] ([Year]:[Number])
- Case Law: NJA [Year] s. [Page] (Supreme Court)
- Sections: X kap. Y § [Law name]

## Legal Terms Glossary

### Common Terms Across Jurisdictions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Burden of Proof** | Obligation to prove allegations/charges |
| **Damages** | Monetary compensation for harm |
| **Defendant/Respondent** | Party being sued or accused |
| **Discovery** | Pre-trial exchange of evidence |
| **Injunction** | Court order requiring action/restraint |
| **Jurisdiction** | Authority of court to hear case |
| **Plaintiff/Claimant** | Party bringing the lawsuit |
| **Precedent** | Prior court decision used as authority |
| **Prima Facie** | Sufficient evidence unless rebutted |
| **Standing** | Legal right to bring lawsuit |
| **Statute of Limitations** | Time limit for filing claims |
